# The Moon Finally Answers Back

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) entry: one line in the voice of the Moon, finally answering the wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

`main.ts` writes exactly one line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> No, I will not make him text back. I am the Moon, not your ex's mother.
